# Broker quirks - IC Markets

- Symbols have no suffix on the Raw account; gold is XAUUSD.
- Gold spread is about 10 points in London but 40-60 points around US news.
- Swap on gold longs is negative; avoid holding gold over the weekend.
- Server time is GMT+2 (GMT+3 in summer), so the daily candle opens at 00:00 server time.
