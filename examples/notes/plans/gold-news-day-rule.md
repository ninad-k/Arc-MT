# Gold news-day rule

On high-impact USD news days (CPI, NFP, FOMC) I do not open new XAUUSD trades from 30 minutes before
the release until 30 minutes after it.

Why: spreads on gold widen to 40-60 points at my broker (see [[Broker quirks - IC Markets]]) and the
first move often reverses. Lesson learned the hard way in [[Lesson - NFP whipsaw]].

If a position is already open, tighten the stop loss to break-even before the release or close half.
