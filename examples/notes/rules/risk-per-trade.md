# Risk per trade

Never risk more than 1% of equity on one trade. Two open trades at most on correlated pairs
(EURUSD and GBPUSD count as one).

- Size the lot from the stop distance, never the other way round.
- Daily loss limit: 2%. Weekly: 4%. When hit, stop and write a note.
- Arc MT's max_lot limit is a hard cap on top of this, not a target.
