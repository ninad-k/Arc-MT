# Pre-trade checklist

1. Is there a high-impact event in the next hour? Check the calendar (and the Gold news-day rule for
   gold).
2. Is the spread normal for this hour?
3. Is the stop loss at a level that invalidates the idea, not at a round number?
4. Is the size within [[Risk per trade]]?
5. Can I say the reason for the trade in one sentence?

If any answer is no, skip the trade.
